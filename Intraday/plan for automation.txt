Paper simulation → stable workflow → dashboard confidence → manual live micro-trades → semi-automation → full automation



Before any automatic buying/selling, the system needs to prove:

1. Signals are correct
2. Paper trades are created correctly
3. Open trades are updated correctly
4. Stop/target/EOD logic works every time
5. Power BI refresh is stable
6. No duplicate trades
7. No accidental overnight holds
8. No wrong ticker selection
9. No stale data used for decisions
10. Every decision is logged
