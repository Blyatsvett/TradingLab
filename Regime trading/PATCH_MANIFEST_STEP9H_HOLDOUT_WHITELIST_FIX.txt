STEP 9H HOLDOUT EXECUTION WHITELIST FIX

Problem
-------
Step 9H correctly generated holdout point-in-time characteristics, but the
shared build_market_state helper still resolved the frozen Step 9B discovery
universe whitelist in the Step 9B module namespace. All 18 locked holdout
symbols were therefore removed before contract eligibility, yielding 0
eligible rows despite complete holdout data.

Fix
---
- Step 9H temporarily injects the locked 18-symbol holdout universe into the
  Step 9B market-state helper only while the holdout experiment executes.
- The original Step 9B ticker whitelist is restored in a finally block.
- The frozen discovery universe, production system, strategies, contract
  definitions, thresholds, and holdout lock are unchanged.
- Step 9H multiple-testing metadata now correctly names the three locked
  primary holdout contracts rather than the seven-contract Step 9G family.
- Added regression coverage proving a holdout ticker reaches eligibility and
  that the original whitelist is restored after execution.

Validation
----------
109 tests passed.
