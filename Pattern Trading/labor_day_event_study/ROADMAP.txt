What comes after the macro registry

Then we leave infrastructure work and start the actual market research:

Phase 1 — Tradable universe and prices
finalize securities for refiners, auto dealers and domestic leisure;
finalize benchmarks and sector ETFs;
acquire adjusted OHLCV data;
handle delistings and ticker changes;
validate price completeness across 1998–2025.

Phase 2 — Event-study engine
raw returns;
benchmark-adjusted returns;
market-model abnormal returns;
cumulative abnormal returns for every frozen window;
discovery versus validation samples;
clean versus contaminated years.

Phase 3 — Strategy construction
basket formation;
entry and exit rules;
equal-weight and volatility-weight alternatives;
transaction costs and slippage;
long-only and long/short variants.

Phase 4 — Robustness
leave-one-year-out tests;
alternative benchmarks;
placebo holidays and random dates;
multiple-testing controls;
sensitivity to windows and outliers;
pre- versus post-structural-break comparisons.

Phase 5 — 2026 execution plan
determine which hypotheses survived validation;
identify applicable 2026 windows;
incorporate known 2026 contamination;
produce actual watchlists, entries, exits and risk limits.
